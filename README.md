# Background of ZenSchola
As a collage student, I'm sure you often think of:
- "What are today's lectures?"
- "How is my attendance stats?"
Or maybe you even face several problems, such as:
- Too much links — Attendance, Lectures URLs, Personal Study Apps
- Scattered Personal Study Apps and University's Softwares
- Sudden unexpected information, needs to be noted
- Mixing up lectures rooms, lecturer, and time

That problems also annoyed the creator of ZenSchola — Lucas. So, he came up with ideas "Why not make an app to centralizes all that?"

# ZenSchola
ZenSchola, daily lectures details, study software apps, university apps, study utilities, student performance tracker, in one app. With this app, we're trying to help junior college student to manage their daily activities. ZenSchola only supports Informatics Engineering at Soegijapranata Catholic University, well.. for now.

# Features
## Attendance trackers
ZenSchola could track your attendance per lectures. It's data depends on the count of you clicking the "Present" button. ZenSchola tracks all of them then converts them into line graph for you to be easy-readed.

ZenSchola will also show you today's lectures, and which lectures in that day you've attended, available for attendance, and missed.

## Daily Lectures
Take a look at today's lectures — Upcoming, ongoing, or ended. On upcoming and ongoing, you'll be able to open attendance page, Cyber page, and your own study app, per lectures.

## Study Utilities
Put all of study softwares you need in one shelf. Simple, Quick Access.

## Scratch Pad
Click once, then type, done. Don't bother clicking save button, its autosave. The Scratch Pad was made simple on purpose. Scratch Pad can be used to type a sudden information or a temporary notes.

## Student ID
At some moment, we need our Student ID QR code. And sometimes, we need it quick. So, Student ID in ZenSchola only 2 clicks away, Menu > Student ID. Don't bother think of "It's an image, will it consume my storage and mobile data?", 'cause the QR code is natively generated, not saved on the device nor the server.
